# Tone guide

Pick the style the person asks for. When they don't say, use **Warm**.

| Style | Sounds like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm | "Thank you so much for…" | Family and friends |
| Simple | "Thanks for…" | Neighbours, quick notes |
| Formal | "I am very grateful for…" | Work, people you don't know well |

Avoid jokes unless the person asks for a funny note. Never invent details about
the gift or favour; ask instead.
