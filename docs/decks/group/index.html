Sovra · For a family or a small business

A few computers share helpers and memory.

What the desktop helper does today. Then a direction for a small group of people and their computers. The direction is not in the product yet.

The problem

A slow laptop should not send family notes away.

Cloud helpers take the files. A second computer in the house could have done the work.

Today

The helper already runs on the computer.

Sovra is a program on the person’s own machine. People ask it in everyday words.

Today

A saved detail can be opened.

A correction replaces it. The old detail is not erased. It stays as history.

Today

Helpers can answer together as a team.

Each helper has a different job. You ask them in one chat, and they answer together.

Today

Each computer still works alone.

Memory and helpers stay on that machine. A slow laptop cannot borrow a free one nearby.

Next

Make a group of people and their computers.

A family, or a small business. Nothing is shared until someone chooses to share it.

Next

Join with a link or a code.

Scan a code, or type six digits in the same room. No Sovra server has to introduce the computers.

Next

Memory has lanes.

Only me, the group, or you and one person. A private note stays private.

Next

A helper you share still waits until they switch it on.

They see what it will be told and which tools it asks for. Grants stay on their computer.

Next

A busy laptop can hand heavy work to a free computer.

Gran’s old laptop still gets an answer from a computer nearby. The computer that did the work is named on the reply.

Next

Nothing needs a central Sovra server.

The computers talk in private. If one turns off, the others keep going.
